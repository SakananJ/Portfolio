Spark Design Club

Electromechanical Subteam Lead Executive

Spark Design Club is building an arcade, and it needs multiple 300 mm linear mechanisms that can actuate quickly under load without exceeding the cabinet's power budget. I am leading the electromechanical subteam that is designing, building, and testing them.

I prototyped rack-and-pinion and belt-driven designs in SolidWorks, driving each with NEMA 17 steppers and worm gear motors. The worm gear motor running at 400 RPM allowed the mechanisms to rise at roughly 25 cm/s with the payload attached, covering their full 300 mm of travel in about 1.2 seconds, and the worm gearing holds position at rest without drawing current. I 3D printed and sourced the parts, then bench-tested each revision for travel speed and current draw.

On the electrical side, I wrote the Arduino firmware that ties addressable LED warning sequences to motor control and limit-switch feedback. I built and soldered the harness myself and verified every connection with a digital multimeter before integrating the motors, LEDs, sensors, and control circuitry into a working system (power circuits). I also mentored a subteam member through the development cycle - CAD, wiring, and firmware - so they could carry their own work through to a finished assembly.
